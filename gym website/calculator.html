Personalized Diet Plans

These sample plans are based on common calorie goals. For a custom plan tailored to your specific BMR/TDEE and dietary needs, consult our certified nutritionists.

Weight Loss Focus: Sustainable Fat Reduction

Macro Breakdown: P: 40%, C: 35%, F: 25% (High Protein, Moderate Carb/Fat)

Breakfast (approx 350 kcal)

Example: Oatmeal (1/2 cup dry) with berries and 1 scoop of whey protein.

Veg Swap: Add 2 tbsp of mixed nuts/seeds instead of whey.

Morning Snack (approx 150 kcal)

Example: 1 medium apple with 1 tbsp peanut butter.

Lunch (approx 500 kcal)

Example: Chicken breast salad (150g grilled chicken, large mixed greens, light vinaigrette).

Veg Swap: Lentil soup (1.5 cups) with 1 whole-wheat roti.

Evening Snack (approx 150 kcal)

Example: Small serving of non-fat yogurt or a small protein bar.

Dinner (approx 700 kcal)

Example: 150g baked fish, 1 cup brown rice, and steamed vegetables.

Veg Swap: Tofu/Paneer curry (low-oil) with mixed vegetable sabzi and half a cup of brown rice.

Weight Gain Focus: Muscle & Mass Building

Macro Breakdown: P: 30%, C: 45%, F: 25% (Balanced for Muscle Hypertrophy)

Breakfast (approx 600 kcal)

Example: 3 scrambled eggs, 2 slices whole-wheat toast, 1 large banana, and a glass of milk.

Veg Swap: Large vegetable omelet (4 eggs) with avocado on toast.

Morning Snack (approx 400 kcal)

Example: Mass Gainer shake (milk, 1 scoop protein, oats, peanut butter).

Lunch (approx 800 kcal)

Example: Beef/Mutton mince (200g), 2 full servings of rice/roti, large side salad with oil-based dressing.

Veg Swap: Chickpea and vegetable curry (chana masala), 2 cups rice, and a glass of lassi.

Evening Snack (approx 400 kcal)

Example: Cottage cheese (paneer) with mixed dried fruit and nuts.

Dinner (approx 600 kcal)

Example: Large chicken biryani (2 servings) or large chicken korma with naan.

Veg Swap: Large mixed vegetable pulao with a side of raita and 2 pieces of paneer tikka.
