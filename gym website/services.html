Our Core Fitness Services

A comprehensive list of programs designed to help you achieve any fitness goal.

Strength Training

Build muscle, increase bone density, and boost your metabolism with our fully equipped strength area and guided programs.

Benefits

  • Enhanced muscle mass and definition
  • Increased strength and power
  • Better posture and joint stability

Who it's for: Beginners, seasoned lifters, and anyone looking to improve body composition.

Session Teaser: Free orientation session with every new membership. Personal session prices start from 3,000 PKR.

Cardio Classes & Area

State-of-the-art cardio machines and energetic group classes (Spinning, Aerobics) to improve cardiovascular health and endurance.

Benefits

  • Improved heart and lung health
  • Effective calorie burning for weight loss
  • Stress reduction and mood boost

Who it's for: All fitness levels, especially those focusing on weight management and stamina.

CrossFit / Functional Fitness

High-intensity, constantly varied functional movements performed in a community setting. Focuses on overall conditioning.

Benefits

  • Exceptional full-body conditioning
  • Strong sense of community and motivation
  • Scalable for any experience level

Who it's for: Individuals seeking diverse, challenging workouts and group accountability.

Session Teaser: Daily WOD (Workout of the Day) sessions. Specialty classes require Pro/Premium membership.

Yoga & Pilates

Improve flexibility, balance, and core strength. Our studios offer Vinyasa, Hatha, and Reformer Pilates.

Benefits

  • Increased flexibility and range of motion
  • Improved core stability and muscle tone
  • Mind-body connection and stress relief

Personal Training (1:1)

Dedicated one-on-one sessions with a certified expert focusing entirely on your specific goals, injuries, and schedule.

Benefits

  • Truly customized training plan and diet advice
  • Maximum accountability and motivation
  • Faster, safer progress towards goals
